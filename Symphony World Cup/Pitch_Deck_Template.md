# Symphony AI World Cup — Pitch Deck Template

Squads must complete this template for their Match Day presentation. All slides are mandatory. The deck is designed to be presented in 10 minutes with 5 minutes of Q&A from the Scouts.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

| Field | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| Squad Name | Your squad's name |
| Solution / Problem Title | A clear, punchy title for what you built or solved |
| Round | Round number (1, 2, 3, or 4) |
| Match Day Date | The date of your presentation |
| Disciplines | Confirm your squad covers Design · Product · Engineering / Data · Business SME |

---

## Slide 2 — The Squad

One entry per squad member. Complete all fields.

**For each squad member:**
- Full name
- Job title
- Region
- Discipline (Design / Product / Engineering or Data / Business SME)
- My contribution — what did you bring to this squad? What did you own?

---

## Slide 3 — The Problem / Opportunity

**The Problem or Opportunity**
Describe the problem or opportunity in plain language. What is broken, slow, missing, or unexplored? Be specific — a vague problem produces a vague solution. A Scout should understand this immediately.

**Why It Matters**
Who is affected? What does it cost the business in time, money, or quality? What is the scale of the problem?

**SME Validated**
Name and role of your SME. Confirm that this problem is real and significant from the business perspective.

---

## Slide 4a — As-Is Process *(Process Improvement Squads Only)*

> Use this slide if you are improving an existing process. Net new idea squads use Slide 4b instead. Delete this instruction before presenting.

**Process Steps** — Map out the current process across up to five steps. For each step include:
- Who performs it
- How long it takes
- What tool or system is used

**Baseline Metrics:**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total time | [X hrs/week] |
| People involved | [X people] |
| Frequency | [Daily / Weekly / Monthly] |
| Estimated cost | [cost / hrs] |

*This baseline is what your results on Slide 7 will be measured against. Your SME must sign off on these numbers.*

---

## Slide 4b — Opportunity Definition *(Net New Idea Squads Only)*

> Use this slide if you are building something that did not exist before. Process improvement squads use Slide 4a instead. Delete this instruction before presenting.

**The Gap**
What is currently missing or not possible? What does the business have to do without, or work around?

**The Evidence**
Why do you believe this gap is real and significant? What data, feedback, or direct observation supports this?

**SME Context**
What does your SME add? Confirm from the business perspective why this gap matters and what solving it is worth.

---

## Slide 5 — The Solution

**What We Built**
Describe the solution clearly. What does it do? What problem does it solve or new capability does it create? Avoid jargon — a senior business leader should understand this in 30 seconds.

**How AI Was Used**
List the AI tools used and specifically how. What did AI enable that would not have been possible otherwise? Be concrete:
- Not: *"we used AI to help"*
- Yes: *"we used [tool] to [do X], which [achieved Y]"*

**Screenshot or Diagram**
Insert a screenshot, architecture diagram, or visual of your solution.

---

## Slide 6 — Live Demo

*No content to prepare for this slide — it is a section marker.*

Run your solution live during this section. Walk the Scouts through a real use case using actual data. Reserve 2–3 minutes of your 10-minute slot for the live demonstration.

---

## Slide 7 — The Results

**Three Key Metrics** — pick the numbers that best demonstrate the value created. Results must come from actual usage during the round, not projections. Your SME must validate all numbers.

| Metric | Value | Unit | What it measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary metric | [X] | [unit] | [what this measures and why it matters most] |
| Secondary metric | [X] | [unit] | [supporting evidence of value] |
| Third metric | [X] | [unit] | [any further measure of impact] |

**How Results Were Measured**
How long did the solution run? On what data or workflows? How were the numbers calculated? Be specific enough that a Scout could verify the methodology.

**SME Sign-Off**
SME name confirms these results are genuine and accurately represent the business impact.

---

## Slide 8 — The New State

**How It Works Now**
Describe the new process or capability step by step. Be clear enough that someone who was not on your squad could take this and run it without you.

- *For process improvement squads:* what replaced the old steps?
- *For net new squads:* what can now be done that could not be done before?

**Before** *(summarise in 2–3 points)*
What was the old state, or what was the gap?

**After** *(summarise in 2–3 points)*
What is the new state?

---

## Slide 9 — Scalability

**Who Else Benefits?**
Which other teams, regions, or clients could use this? How many people or processes could this affect if scaled across Symphony?

**What Would It Take?**
What needs to happen to scale this? Dependencies, integrations, or resources required? Keep it realistic and honest.

**Potential Wider Impact**
If this scaled across Symphony or to clients, what is the estimated impact? Use the same metrics as your results slide.

---

## Slide 10 — The Ask

**To Implement This, We Need**
What resources, access, decisions, or support does this require to go from here to live across the business?

**The Solution Owner After Today**
Who will own this solution once the competition ends? Name the person and the squad. Typically the SME is best placed.

**The First Next Step**
What is the single most important action in the next two weeks to keep this moving? Name the action and the owner.

---

## Presentation Guidelines

**Time**
- Round Match Day: 10 minutes to present, 5 minutes Q&A
- Grand Final Event: format set by the central owner based on finalist count

**What the Scouts will ask**
Squads are encouraged to use AI to prepare for these questions before Match Day — and The Friendly with your Squad Coach is designed to surface them early:
- How did you establish the as-is baseline or define the opportunity?
- How long did the solution actually run and on what data?
- What happens if this breaks?
- Who owns this after today?
- What would it cost to scale across the business?

**Mandatory deliverables**
Every submission must include all of the following or will be disqualified:
1. A working solution — built, deployed, and running on real data
2. Measured results — from actual usage, validated by the SME
3. The new state — how things work now
4. As-is documentation *(process improvement squads)* or opportunity definition *(net new squads)*

**Tips**
- Use AI to stress-test your pitch, simulate Scout questions, and sharpen your narrative before Match Day
- Your Squad Coach will run The Friendly before Match Day — treat it seriously, it is the closest thing to the real event
- Every squad receives a written Match Report from the Scouts within 48 hours of Match Day, win or lose
